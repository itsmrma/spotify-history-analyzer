The visual language is intentionally Material rather than a replacement brand.
Product specificity comes from music rankings, streaks, activity, missing metadata
and duration estimates. Strengths include the single import action, consistent
outlined fields, meaningful empty states, accessible chart equivalents, keyboard
calendar, cancellable requests and preservation of existing data on failure.

The entry flow had low cognitive load but an emotional valley when users did not
know where to obtain their files or whether importing was private. The dashboard
presented three overview metrics, three winners and six facts before the first
chart; the facts are now expandable. The five jump links remain useful direct
navigation rather than an artificial four-item limit.

Persona checks: Alex benefits from batch import and undo; Sam from readable labels,
visible focus and keyboard alternatives; Casey from larger calendar targets,
mobile input sizing and explicit session behavior.
